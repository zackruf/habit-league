# Development Roadmap

## Current MVP

The current MVP includes:

- authentication and profile basics
- golf-group creation and joining
- course search foundation and saved course records
- individual stroke and scramble round logging
- course-specific leaderboard foundations
- golf-group chat
- activity feed and shoutouts
- friend requests and discovery
- personal best surfaces
- older streak restore and boosts foundations kept stable in the background

## Near-Term Priorities

- course database provider expansion
- richer round scorecards and hole-by-hole editing
- stronger course and group leaderboard summaries
- onboarding polish for faster first-round setup
- notification/readiness foundations
- clearer weekly summaries and score-movement moments
- social discovery expansion

## Medium-Term Priorities

- real provider-backed course lookup
- friend and group invite flows
- public-vs-friends leaderboard polish
- handicap tracking and badges
- development-build purchase testing and billing validation
- production hardening for Firebase rules, error states, and release workflow

## Future / Advanced Features

- richer social identity and profile previews
- photo sharing for round moments
- improved golf-group administration
- deeper monetization and entitlements
- scramble event tools and league formats
- analytics, monitoring, and release observability
- notification delivery and lifecycle automation

## Likely Next Steps

- Course database integration polish
- Real hole-by-hole scoring and scorecard editing
- Public vs friends leaderboard polish
- Personal best and handicap expansion
- Notifications/readiness
- Onboarding polish
- Social discovery expansion
- Friend/group invites
- Purchase testing with dev builds
- Production hardening
