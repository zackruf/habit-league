# Development Roadmap

## Current MVP

The current MVP includes:

- authentication and profile basics
- league creation and joining
- shared league challenges
- per-user challenge participation records
- daily check-ins
- weekly rankings
- league chat
- activity feed and shoutouts
- friend requests and discovery
- streak restore and boosts foundations
- challenge lifecycle basics

## Near-Term Priorities

- challenge lifecycle polish
- better owner flows for ending and rotating challenges
- onboarding polish for faster league entry
- notification/readiness foundations
- clearer weekly summaries and urgency moments
- social discovery expansion

## Medium-Term Priorities

- shared challenge templates and faster challenge setup
- friend and group invite flows
- public league discovery improvements
- stronger recap and re-engagement surfaces
- development-build purchase testing and billing validation
- production hardening for Firebase rules, error states, and release workflow

## Future / Advanced Features

- richer social identity and profile previews
- improved league administration
- deeper monetization and entitlements
- more advanced challenge rotation flows
- analytics, monitoring, and release observability
- notification delivery and lifecycle automation

## Likely Next Steps

- Group challenge lifecycle polish
- Notifications/readiness
- Shared challenge templates
- Onboarding polish
- Social discovery expansion
- Friend/group invites
- Purchase testing with dev builds
- Production hardening
